# Country flags used in the passport header

`FlagVietnam.png` and `FlagKorea.png` are 384 × 256 PNG sprites resized from the 960 × 640 previews of these Wikimedia Commons public-domain flag files:

- Vietnam: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Vietnam.svg
- Republic of Korea: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_South_Korea.svg

The original flag files are listed as public domain on their respective file pages. Their designs were also checked against the national-flag descriptions from the Vietnam Government Portal and the Republic of Korea Ministry of the Interior and Safety:

- https://vietnam.gov.vn/national-flag-emblem-anthem-declaration-of-independence-68960
- https://www.mois.go.kr/eng/sub/a03/nationalSymbol/screen.do

Downloaded and resized for Taste Passport on 2 November 2026. The sprites appear only as small header marks; they are not full-screen backgrounds.
